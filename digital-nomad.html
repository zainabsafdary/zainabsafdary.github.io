Remote Work Mobility and Host-Country Characteristics: An Empirical Analysis of Digital Nomad Destination Choice.

Mapping cities that are affordable,safe, and have fast internet speed.

Digital nomads are people who use technology to work remotely and live a location independent lifestyle, but choosing where to live means balancing cost, safety, and internet quality. Compiling data on 151 cities from Nomadlist , I plotted monthly essential costs against internet speed to find cities that are affordable, safe, and well connected. Cities that beat the median on cost, safety, and internet speed are highlighted in blue, with the "sweet spot" shaded in the upper left of the chart. Bucharest, Granada, Osaka, and Florianópolis stand out for pairing fast internet with monthly costs near $1,000.
